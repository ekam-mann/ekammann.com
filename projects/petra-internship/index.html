Petra Engineers and Consultants

Mechanical design engineering internship in oil and gas: pipe stress, redlining, and code based calcs.

Scope: Mechanical Design Engineering Intern, oil and gas, Calgary. Tools: CAEPIPE, AutoCAD. Standards: ASME B31.3, CSA Z662, ASME B16.5.

As a mechanical design engineering intern at Petra Engineers and Consultants in Calgary, I supported piping and facility design work on operating oil and gas infrastructure under senior engineer supervision. The work was grounded in industry codes and real project deliverables:

  • Ran pipe stress analysis in CAEPIPE for a natural gas distribution system, interpreting code stress results against allowables and flagging 6 support locations for senior engineering review.
  • Redlined 4 engineering drawing packages (P&IDs, site plans, demolition plans) against ASME B31.3, CSA Z662, and ASME B16.5, producing 11 review comments that were incorporated into issued revisions.
  • Calculated minimum required wall thickness for 12 line segments using a CSA Z662 design spreadsheet, producing summaries used in project reports and construction drawings.
  • Verified bills of materials, equipment lists, and valve lists against AutoCAD isometrics for a regulator station scope, correcting 14 discrepancies across 87 line items, and logged 7 missing or illegible equipment tags during site visits for as-built updates.
